Sources and how much to trust them

  1. Duty, China framed mirrors (HTS 7009.92): 6.5% base rate and 25% Section 301 confirmed by US Customs ruling NY N326194. Primary source.
  2. 12.5% forced-labor duty (since Jul 24 2026): reported by NNR Global Logistics and Airlift USA, citing 91 FR 47318. Freight companies, which benefit when importers worry about tariffs; check the Federal Register notice before ordering.
  3. Vietnam rate: Airlift USA, same bias as above.
  4. Amazon Small Bulky tier and fees: tier limits from Amazon (primary); fee rates from GOAT Consulting, an agency that sells Amazon services. Confirm in Seller Central's revenue calculator.
  5. Ocean freight: ExFreight, Ship4wd, Topway. All are forwarders quoting their own market; ranges disagree by 3×.

Not verified: whether the lit mirror is classified as a mirror (7009) or a lamp (9405), and whether Section 232 applies to the aluminum frame. A licensed customs broker can answer both for about $100–200.
