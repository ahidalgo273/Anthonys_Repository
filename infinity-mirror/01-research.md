# Infinity-Border Mirror — Phase 1 Research (2026-11-02)

Concept: plug-in rectangular mirror. Center = normal mirror. Border = thin RGB infinity-mirror strips.

## Findings
1. **Direct competitor exists.** Nova of California "Carnival" 36" round LED infinity mirror — normal mirror center, infinity ring border, plug-in, ships with a smart plug. Also sold: "Starfall Vanishing Infinity" round mirror. Our idea is not new; differentiation must come from shape, price, size, or features.
2. **Factory pricing (LED bathroom mirrors, not infinity):** $40–90 FOB, MOQ 1–100 units, 25–45 day lead time (Global Sources listings, several dated 2023 or earlier).
3. **Certification is the largest fixed cost.** Lighted mirrors are certified to UL 962. Major US marketplaces expect NRTL (UL/ETL) certification. UL warned in Oct 2024 about fake UL marks on LED bathroom mirrors. Vendor estimate: $15k–40k+ and 12–20 weeks for a US+EU package (US-only should be lower; not verified).
4. **Low-voltage design option:** a separately listed Class 2 external adapter (UL 8750 / UL 1310) keeps mains voltage out of the mirror. Whether Amazon accepts that in place of a full-product listing is NOT verified.
5. **Tariffs on China goods (as of Nov 2026):** IEEPA tariffs struck down Feb 20 2026. Since Jul 24 2026: 12.5% Section 301 duty on most Chinese goods, plus 25% if the HTS code is on Lists 1–3 → ~37.5% typical. HTS 7009 (mirrors) list status not yet verified.
6. **Marketplace fees:** Amazon referral ~15%; FBA Large Bulky ~$9–10 base + ~$0.38–0.43/lb (third-party figures). eBay Home & Garden final value fee 12.7% (eBay, Jan 2025) + ~$0.30–0.40/order.
7. **Patents:** base infinity-mirror patent US 4,761,004 (1988) is expired. Portal Infinity Mirrors holds design patent D1024833 (granted 2024-04-30) — claim not yet reviewed. Need a freedom-to-operate check.
